## Architecture map

`Program` → `AppInitializationService` → `FrmStartup` on an empty database or `FrmLogin` on an initialized database → `FrmMain` → `FrmRecords`, `FrmReportCenter`, `FrmSettings`, and protected dialogs. The legacy WinForms/services path remains the normal operational path. `Application/*`, `Core/*`, and `UI/Composition/Pilot/*` contain the newer target contracts, SQLite boundaries, shadow/pilot composition, and authority gates; they are substantially test-covered but are not yet the complete replacement for every legacy operational path.

## Interpretation

The Pilot baseline is valid, but “complete polished industrial product” is not. The major blockers are active legacy-path security/data-safety defects, the exposed test seeder, incorrect active runtime/event behavior, the absent installer/data-location design, incomplete target-security composition, and unperformed real UI acceptance. Target-layer tests prove useful safety foundations; they do not close defects in the legacy UI/services that operators can reach.
